About Me

My name is Handong Zhao. I am a Senior Research Scientist at Adobe Research in San Jose, CA. My research focuses on vision-language foundation models, multimodal agents, and post-training, with applications in visual understanding, generation, and tool-using creative AI systems. At Adobe, I have led and co-led research efforts on multimodal image editing agents, large-scale vision-language foundation models, and image/video generation post-training, with technologies transferred across Adobe Creative Cloud products.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University , Boston, MA. I was also a recipient of the National Science Foundation Innovation Corps (I-Corps™) award as an Entrepreneurial Lead.
